# Privacy Policy - Statgol

**Last updated: 17 September 2026**

Statgol ("the app") is published by **AppVita Studio**. This page explains what
data the app handles and why.

## Summary

Statgol is a football statistics and prediction app. It downloads public match
results from the internet and keeps them on your device, so it also works
offline. It does not require an account, it does not ask for your name, e-mail
address or phone number, and it does not contain a login.

## Data collected by the app itself

**None.** All settings you choose (language, light or dark theme, favourite
teams, whether you accepted the disclaimer, ad-free periods) are stored **only
on your device** and are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Statgol has no user accounts, no chat, no user-generated content, and does not
collect contacts, photos, files, precise location or health data.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Statgol is free and is funded by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**
(Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To show ads, the AdMob SDK may collect
and share:

* the **advertising ID** of your device (a resettable identifier);
* **approximate location** derived from your IP address (country / city level,
  not precise GPS location);
* technical information about the device and the app (device model, operating
  system version, app version, language, ad interaction events).

This data is used for **analytics** and for **advertising and marketing**,
including measuring and, where allowed, personalising ads.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows
a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) **before** loading any ads.
You can choose whether to allow personalised ads and you can change your choice
at any time from **Settings** inside the app. If you do not consent,
non-personalised ads may still be shown, or no ads at all.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID from your device settings
(Settings > Google > Ads on most Android phones).

## Football data downloaded from the internet

Match results shown in the app come from public, freely available datasets
published by third parties:

* **openfootball** (`raw.githubusercontent.com` and `cdn.jsdelivr.net`), an open
  dataset of fixtures and results, used as the main source;
* **football-data.co.uk**, public CSV files that also contain closing betting
  odds, used as a secondary source.

The app downloads the file for the selected league automatically when the data
it already has is more than 12 hours old, and whenever you ask for an update.
The downloaded data is stored **only on your device** so that the app keeps
working offline, and the date of the last download is shown inside the app.

Only a plain HTTP request is made: **no personal data is sent**. As with any
internet request, the third-party server may record technical information such
as your IP address and the type of device making the request, according to its
own policy.

## Sharing and PDF export

If you choose to share a prediction or export it as a PDF, the content is passed
to the app you select (messaging app, e-mail, printer, cloud storage). Statgol
does not send that content anywhere on its own and keeps no copy of it.

## Children

Statgol deals with football predictions and betting odds and is intended for
**adults only (18+)**. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly
collect data from children.

## Responsible gambling

Statgol provides statistical estimates for information and entertainment. It
does not accept bets, does not handle money and is not affiliated with any
bookmaker. No result is guaranteed. If gambling is causing you problems, seek
help from a specialised service in your country.

## Data retention and your rights

Since Statgol itself does not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to
retain or delete. For data processed by Google AdMob, please refer to Google's
privacy policy linked above; you can exercise your rights, including withdrawing
consent, through the in-app privacy settings and your device settings.
